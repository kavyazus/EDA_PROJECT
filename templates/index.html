🔬 Key EDA Insights & Conclusions

01

Streams are heavily right-skewed

A small number of mega-hits account for a disproportionate share of total streams. The median is far below the mean — the "superstar effect" is real.

02

Energy ↔ Acousticness: Negative Correlation

Higher energy songs are almost never acoustic. This confirms that electronic/produced tracks dominate the streaming charts.

03

Major key & ~120 BPM dominates

The sweet spot for a charting song: Major key, 100–130 BPM, high danceability, and high energy. Listeners prefer upbeat, danceable music.

04

January & May: Prime release months

Artists strategically release in January (new year buzz) and May (summer prep). These months have the highest number of charting releases.

05

Spotify far outpaces other platforms

Top songs appear in 10× more Spotify playlists than Apple Music or Deezer — Spotify's algorithmic curation is unmatched.

06

Older songs accumulate more streams

Tracks from 2017–2020 average higher streams despite fewer new releases — they've had years to grow through replays and playlist additions.
